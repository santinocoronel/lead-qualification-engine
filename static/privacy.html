Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 22, 2026

This is a plain-language draft covering the essentials for launch. If you serve users in the EU/UK, California, or other jurisdictions with specific data-protection statutes (GDPR, CCPA, etc.), have this reviewed against those requirements before relying on it.

1. What we collect

DataWhy
Email address, hashed passwordAccount creation and login
Hashed platform API key (X-API-Key)Authenticating programmatic and dashboard requests
Encrypted AI provider key (only if you opt in via Settings → BYOK)Convenience — so you don't have to paste it every time
Plan tier, monthly usage countersEnforcing plan quotas and billing
Task descriptions and code you submit for generation/reviewForwarded to the AI provider you selected to produce a response; not retained by us beyond what's needed to stream the result back to you
Request logs (timestamps, request IDs, endpoint, status code)Debugging, abuse prevention, and reliability
Billing identifiers from our payment processorMatching your subscription status; we do not receive or store your card number

2. What we don't do

  • We do not use your prompts, code, or generated output to train any AI model, ours or anyone else's.
  • We do not sell your personal data.
  • We do not use tracking cookies. Session state is kept in your browser's local storage (your JWT session token and, if you're on the dashboard, your platform API key), not in cookies.

3. Who we share data with

  • The AI provider you choose (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, DeepSeek, or Mistral) receives your task description or code and your provider API key, directly, to generate a response. Their own privacy policy governs how they handle that data.
  • Payment processors (Lemon Squeezy and/or PayPro Global) handle checkout and billing as merchant of record; they receive what's needed to process your subscription.
  • Resend, our transactional email provider, sends account emails (welcome, password reset, usage alerts) on our behalf.
  • We do not share data with anyone else except where required by law.

4. Security

Passwords are hashed with bcrypt. Platform API keys are stored as SHA-256 hashes, never in plaintext. An optionally-saved AI provider key is encrypted with Fernet (AES-128) before it's written to the database. All traffic is encrypted in transit via TLS.

5. Data retention

We retain account data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we delete your account record and any saved BYOK key within a reasonable period, except where we're required to retain billing records for tax or legal purposes.

6. Your rights

You can access, correct, or delete your account data, and remove a saved BYOK key at any time from Settings. To request a full export or deletion of your account, contact us using the address in your dashboard. If you're in the EU/UK, you may also have rights to restrict or object to processing under GDPR; contact us to exercise them.

7. Children's privacy

The Service is not directed at children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

8. International transfers

Our infrastructure and the AI providers you may select can process data outside your home country. By using the Service, you consent to that transfer.

9. Changes to this policy

We'll update this page when our practices change and, for material changes, make reasonable efforts to notify active account holders by email.

10. Contact

Privacy questions or requests: use the support address in your account dashboard.
