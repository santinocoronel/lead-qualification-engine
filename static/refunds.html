Refund Policy

Last updated: September 22, 2026

This is a plain-language draft covering the essentials for launch. Consumer-protection law in some jurisdictions (e.g. EU distance-selling rules) may require specific withdrawal-right language beyond what's here — have this reviewed for the markets you sell into.

1. Free plan

The Free plan costs nothing, so there's nothing to refund. You can use it indefinitely within its monthly quota.

2. First-time subscribers: 7-day money-back guarantee

If you're subscribing to a paid plan (Pro or Agency) for the first time and it's not for you, contact us within 7 days of your first charge for a full refund, no questions asked. Refunds under this guarantee are issued to your original payment method through our payment processor and typically appear within 5–10 business days, depending on your bank.

3. After the first 7 days

Subscriptions renew monthly. You can cancel at any time from your dashboard, and cancellation takes effect at the end of your current billing period — you keep access to your paid quota until then. We don't offer prorated refunds for partial months after the initial 7-day window, except where required by law.

4. What's not covered

  • Costs charged directly by an AI provider (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, DeepSeek, Mistral) for your own API key usage. That charge goes straight from you to the provider — we never receive that money, so we can't refund it.
  • Accounts suspended for violating our Terms of Service.

5. How to request a refund

Email the support address listed in your account dashboard with your account email and the reason for the request. We aim to respond within 2 business days.

6. Chargebacks

If you believe you were charged in error, please contact us first — we can usually resolve it faster than a chargeback, and a chargeback may result in your account being suspended while it's investigated by our payment processor.
